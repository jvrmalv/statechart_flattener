### Session 3: Eliminating Q-Learning Blockers in `_04` and `_05`
- **Date**: March 23, 2026
- **Session ID**: `17277ee9-e545-47de-9fcb-71de3083d402` (113 steps)
- **Git Commit**: `af06c2f`
- **Problem**: Downstream Q-learning agents crashed when consuming `_04_LightAndMotionSensingLightSwitch_flattened.json` and `_05_PresenceSimulationLightSwitch_flattened.json` due to three critical defects:
  1. **242 broken transition targets in `_05`**: Transitions calling `get_hour()` or `get_rand(...)` evaluated to `false`, creating targets ending in `__hour_false` that did not exist in the `states` map.
  2. **121 `null` transition inputs in `_05`**: Eventless transitions from `.choice_0` pseudo-states had `"input": null` in JSON, breaking Q-table action indexing.
  3. **22 broken transition targets in `_04`**: `_HISTORY_modes_Automatic_` was replaced before `_HISTORY_modes_Automatic_Automatic.entry_0_`, corrupting the longer marker into `modes_Automatic_Motion_Sensing_StillAutomatic.entry_0_`.
- **Breakthroughs**:
  1. Changed `evaluate_expression/3` fallback from `false` to `:error`; when `execute_actions/5` sees `:error`, it preserves the source state's variable value instead of corrupting it to `false`.
  2. Normalized all `nil` / empty transition events to `"_auto"`.
  3. Sorted `history_defaults` keys by descending string length (`Enum.sort_by(fn {k, _} -> String.length(k) end, :desc)`) before string replacement.
  4. **Result**: **0 broken targets and 0 `null` events** across all 5 LightSwitch models (`_01` through `_05`).

### Session 4: The 7-Phase Enhancement for Harel's Citizen Quartz Watch
- **Date**: April 4, 2026
- **Session ID**: `85b03d08-39a9-469d-afa8-38c605d62b9f` (193 steps)
- **Git Commits**: `746143f`, `8e82cc4`
- **Problem**: Attempting to flatten [harel_watch/watch.scxml](file:////wsl.localhost/Ubuntu-24.04/home/jvrmalv/code/statechart_flattener/harel_watch/watch.scxml) and [harel_watch/watch_subdiagrams.scxml](file:////wsl.localhost/Ubuntu-24.04/home/jvrmalv/code/statechart_flattener/harel_watch/watch_subdiagrams.scxml) caused the compiler to hang indefinitely (>1.4M states) and fail on 7 structural patterns not present in the LightSwitch suite.
- **Breakthroughs (The 7 Phases)**:
  - **Phase 1 — Variable Classification (`classify_variables/2`)**: Separated the 14 `<data>` variables into 7 guard-relevant variables and 7 display-only variables, cutting the variable state factor by $128\times$.
  - **Phase 2 — `In(...)` State Predicate Support**: Added `evaluate_in_predicates/2` to resolve `In('on')` and `In('zero')`.
  - **Phase 3 — Anonymous Root Parallel (`wrap_root_if_parallel/1`)**: Automatically wrapped top-level sibling `<state>` regions (`modes`, `chime`, `light`) into `<parallel id="_root">`.
  - **Phase 4 — Global Suffix Target Fallback**: Extended `resolve_with_scope/3` so cross-diagram references in `watch_subdiagrams.scxml` resolve accurately.
  - **Phase 5 — Deep History & Nested Parallel Defaults**: Updated `extract_history_defaults/3` to resolve composite/parallel defaults inside `<history type="deep">`.
  - **Phase 6 — Bottom-Up Multi-Level `<parallel>` Composition**: Implemented `find_parallel_states_bottom_up/1` so inner `Operation` (4 composites) is flattened before outer `_root` (64 composites).
  - **Phase 7 — Nearest-Value Domain Snapping (`snap_to_domain/2`)**: Mapped large modulo increments (`+ 6000 % 8640000`) onto the finite domain `[0, 1]`.
  - **Result**: Both `watch.scxml` and `watch_subdiagrams.scxml` compiled in **~10 seconds**, producing **7,040 reachable states** and **69,248 transitions** with 0 broken targets.

### Session 6: Eliminating Synthetic Non-Determinism & Theoretical Limits Analysis
- **Date**: April 20–28, 2026
- **Session ID**: `e1045a47-1345-4857-bfcd-067f8cb73cd1` (240 steps)
- **Git Commits**: `8c20bf4`, `2e88cae`, `9bfe8fe`
- **Problem**:
  1. `watch_flattened.json` had **4,480 non-deterministic states** (out of 7,040) where parent transitions (e.g., `c_button` on `Alarm_1`) were not shadowed by child transitions (`c_button` on `Alarm_1_set`), or where `if/else` transitions on the same event both fired.
  2. `TrafficLightCtrl_flattened.json` had non-deterministic `_auto` transitions because `In('Green')` was not evaluated on non-parallel states before pseudo-state chain collapsing.
  3. `Elevator_flattened.json` had duplicate `"input": "submit"` transitions because `_event.data` guards returned `:error` and lost their condition labels.
- **Breakthroughs**:
  1. Implemented **W3C inner-first (`source_depth`) and document-order (`doc_order`) transition priority** in `dedup_by_event_priority/1`.
  2. Enabled `evaluate_in_predicates/2` inside `evaluate_condition/4` for all states (regular and parallel).
  3. Preserved runtime `:error` guards as condition-qualified event labels (`"submit [_event.data > currentFloor]"`).
  4. Conducted a formal theoretical analysis of QuickJS/V8 NIFs vs. the **Halting Problem / Rice's Theorem** for unbounded variables.
  5. **Result**: Non-deterministic states dropped to **0 across every model in the repository** (`watch.scxml`: 4,480 $\to$ **0 ND states**, state count refined from 7,040 to **3,072**).

- **Problem**:
  1. We needed an automated verification suite to prove that `watch_flattened.json` is not merely syntactically valid, but **semantically faithful** to Harel's specification.
  2. Writing [harel_watch/verify_flattened.py](file:////wsl.localhost/Ubuntu-24.04/home/jvrmalv/code/statechart_flattener/harel_watch/verify_flattened.py) and [harel_watch/verify_history.py](file:////wsl.localhost/Ubuntu-24.04/home/jvrmalv/code/statechart_flattener/harel_watch/verify_history.py) immediately exposed two deep semantic gaps in the 3,072-state `watch.scxml` output:
     - **Gap 1 (Static History Amnesia)**: Returning to `Regular` (`deep_entry` / `time_set`) or `Stop_Watch` (`Stop_Watch.entry_0`) always jumped to `Clock_normal` / `Zero`, failing all dynamic history tests.
     - **Gap 2 (0 Alarm Beep States)**: When `time == alarm_1_time` triggered the transition into `Alarm_1_beep` / `chime_beep`, the target state lookup failed to align variable mutations across synchronized parallel transitions, leaving `Alarm_1_beep` unreachable (0 alarm beep states).

Architected and implemented **Stage 9 (`synthesize_history_and_prune/5`)**: a generalized product-automaton BFS ($Q \subseteq S_{\text{atomic}} \times D_{\text{vars}} \times H_{\text{memory}}$) that dynamically tracks shallow and deep history across arbitrary composite and nested parallel states.
  2. Fixed synchronized parallel transition variable resolution so alarm triggers properly reach `beep` states (**6,656 reachable alarm beep states**).
  3. **Result**: `watch.scxml` compiles to **23,040 reachable states and 232,704 transitions**, achieving a **100% pass rate** across all 5 verification checks in `verify_flattened.py` and all 3 history trace suites in `verify_history.py`!
