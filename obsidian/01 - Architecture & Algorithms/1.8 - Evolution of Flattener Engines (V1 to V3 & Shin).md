## 3. Why `scxml_flattener_optimized.exs` and `scxml_flattener_full.exs` Differ on `test_elevator.scxml` and `guards_example.scxml`

A revealing comparison comes from inspecting the historical JSON outputs in `lib/` generated by `scxml_flattener_optimized.exs` (`*_optimized.json`) vs. `scxml_flattener_full.exs` (`*_full.json`):

1. **`lib/guards_example.scxml` (identical to `_03_MotionLightSwitch.scxml`)**:
   - `guards_example_flat.json` (unpruned early V2): **77 states, 165 transitions, 33 `null` inputs**.
   - `guards_example_full.json` (V2-Full with full integer range `[0..10]` for `brightness` + BFS reachability): **43 states, 107 transitions, 0 `null` inputs**.
   - `guards_example_optimized.json` (V2-Opt with sparse boundary domain `[0, 1, 9, 10]`): **13 states, 37 transitions**.
   - Current V3-Full (`scxml_flattener_full.exs` with `snap_to_domain/2` on `[0, 1, 2, 10]`): **7 states**.
2. **`lib/test_elevator.scxml`**:
   - `test_elevator_optimized.json`: **28 states, 60 transitions** (retained unreachable floor combinations).
   - `test_elevator_full.json`: **4 states, 8 transitions** (strictly pruned from `initial="elevator_Operational_Stopped_DoorsOpen__floor_0"`).
3. **`lib/test_vending_machine.scxml`**:
   - `test_vending_machine_full.json` (expanded both `coins` and `hasProduct`): **15 states, 23 transitions**.
   - `test_vending_machine_optimized.json`: **6 states, 10 transitions**.
   - Current V3-Full (classifies `hasProduct` as display-only since it is never used in a `cond`!): **9 states**.
